The russian-place-timezones.tsv.gz resource is derived from the GeoNames
RU country dump, Russian alternate names and administrative code tables,
downloaded on 2026-08-13.

GeoNames data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source and format documentation:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
https://www.geonames.org/

The embedded derivative contains only normalized locality aliases and UTC
offsets. It does not contain coordinates, GeoNames identifiers or FIAS data.
